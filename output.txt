RESEARCH FINDINGS: Origin and Meaning of "kikericsek viritanak kekek es lilak"
================================================================================

PHRASE: "Kikericsek virítanak kékek és lilák"

MEANING (English translation): "Autumn crocuses bloom, blue and purple."

LANGUAGE: Hungarian (translated from French)

ORIGINAL FRENCH TITLE: "Les Colchiques" (Autumn Crocuses)
HUNGARIAN TITLE: "Kikericsek"

ORIGINAL AUTHOR: Guillaume Apollinaire (French poet, 1880–1918)
HUNGARIAN TRANSLATOR: Radnóti Miklós (renowned Hungarian poet)

SOURCE PUBLICATION:
- Original: "Alcools" (1913) — Apollinaire's landmark poetry collection
- Hungarian translation published in: "Száz vers" (One Hundred Poems), edited by Szerb Antal, Budapest, Magvető Kiadó

BACKGROUND / CONTEXT:
The poem "Les Colchiques" was written by Guillaume Apollinaire around 1901–1902, inspired
by Annie Playden, an English governess he met while living in the Rhineland region of Germany.
The poem describes a meadow blooming with poisonous colchicum (autumn crocus) flowers, whose
blue and purple color mirrors the eyes of Apollinaire's beloved. The imagery of the poisonous
yet beautiful flowers serves as a metaphor for a love that slowly 'poisons' or consumes the poet.
It was later published in his celebrated collection "Alcools" in 1913.

The Hungarian translation by Radnóti Miklós faithfully captures the lyrical and melancholic
tone of the original French poem.
